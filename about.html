Who We Are

South Air Helicopters, Inc. is an FAA Certified Repair Station and a Bell Customer Service Facility at Pearland Regional Airport. Bell is our forte.

Robert H. Mitchell founded the company in October 1979, and it became a Bell Customer Service Facility in 1981, which authorizes us to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts. Mike Pike is the company's president today.

We are a maintenance shop rather than an operator. Aircraft come to us for scheduled work, repair, inspection and overhaul.

Our FAA Repair Station certificate is #XRIR622K. See our full credentials.
