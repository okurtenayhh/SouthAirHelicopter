Who We Are

South Air Helicopters, Inc. is an FAA Certified Repair Station and a Bell Customer Service Facility at Pearland Regional Airport, working on the Bell 206 and 407 series, the 429, and the MD 500. Bell is our forte. We have been here since 1979.

Quality and safety is our priority, and our history reflects it.

We are a maintenance shop rather than an operator — aircraft come to us for scheduled work, repair, inspection and overhaul. As a Bell Customer Service Facility since 1981, we are also authorized to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts.
