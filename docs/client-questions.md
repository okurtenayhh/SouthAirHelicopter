# South Air Helicopters — Website Questions

The website is built. Every page exists and works. What's missing is the real information to put in it — right now a lot of the text on the page is a note-to-self in a yellow box, waiting on an answer.

That's what this is. No deadline, and no need to go in order. Skip anything you don't know or don't want on the site. "I don't know" and "leave that off" are both real answers.

A few questions have a ⚠️ next to them. Those are the ones where a guess could cause an actual problem, so they're worth a careful answer rather than a quick one.
