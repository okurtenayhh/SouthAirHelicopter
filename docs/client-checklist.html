Website Checklist
What we need to finish the site

The website is built — every page exists and works. What's missing is the real information to put in it. This is everything still needed, grouped by how it gets gathered. No deadline, and no need to go in order. Skip anything you don't know or don't want on the site — "I don't know" and "leave that off" are both real answers. Items marked Important are ones where a guess could cause an actual problem, so they're worth a careful answer.

If nothing else, these five move the most

  1. Which helicopter models the shop is rated on, and what work on each
  2. One day at the hangar with a camera
  3. The year the company was founded
  4. Bell's seal artwork and written permission to use it
  5. What the NASA work is, in writing

What makes them usable

  • Shot horizontally, at the highest quality setting
  • Sent as originals — texting or WhatsApp shrinks them past the point of use
  • Real photos only, no stock imagery
  • ImportantTail numbers identify an aircraft's owner — worth checking before publishing a customer's aircraft

2Videonothing on hand yet

Shots, in order of value

  • A helicopter lifting off or setting down in the field the main one
  • A mechanic working on an aircraft, shot close
  • A slow move down the length of the shop floor
  • Wide of the hangar, helicopter inside, doors open
  • Two people working a job together
  • The hangar exterior at dawn or dusk

Notes for whoever shoots it

  • Horizontal, highest resolution available, on a tripod or gimbal rather than handheld
  • Let each shot run 30 seconds or more
  • Outdoors is best in the hour after sunrise or before sunset
  • Lock the exposure before recording so it doesn't shift mid‑shot
  • Spinning rotors distort badly on phone cameras — film them at idle, or keep the blades partly out of frame
  • Sound isn't needed; the video plays silently on the site

5Questions

The basics

  • What year was the company founded?
  • Is there a tagline, or a line already used to describe the company?
  • In a sentence or two, what is South Air Helicopters?
  • Is there a mission statement?
  • What are the business hours?
  • What is the airport identifier and the coordinates?
  • Which email address should be the public one?

Aircraft Important

  • Which helicopter models is the shop rated to work on?
  • What work is authorized on each one?
  • Is the shop Bell‑focused, or does it work across manufacturers?

Bell Important

  • Can Bell provide their seal artwork and written permission to use it?
  • What does the Customer Service Facility designation authorize? Does it cover warranty work?
  • Is "Bell Helicopter Customer Service Facility" still the current term? Bell dropped "Helicopter" from the name in 2018
  • How long has the designation been held, and what's required to keep it?
  • Is it acceptable for Bell markings to appear on aircraft in our photographs?

NASA Important

  • What exactly does South Air Helicopters do in connection with NASA?
  • Which center, facility, or program?
  • How much of it can be described publicly, and does NASA need to review it first?
  • When and how did the work begin?

Services and quoting

  • Is the current list of six services correct?
  • What does each one actually include?
  • Is charter or flight support offered at all, or is it maintenance only?
  • What's needed from a customer in order to put a quote together?
  • How does a quote come back to them — a call, an email, a written scope of work?
  • How long does a quote usually take? only worth publishing if it's a number worth being held to
  • Should quote requests come in by phone, through the website, or both?

The company story

  • Who founded the company, and why?
  • What are two or three milestones worth marking, and roughly when?
  • How public should the recent change of ownership be?
  • A short bio for the President
  • Should anyone else appear on the site?
  • Are "Safety First, Reliability, Precision" the right three company values? these were a starting suggestion, not chosen by you

Hiring

  • Are there any open positions right now? "nothing at the moment" is a perfectly good answer
  • For any role: what certificates, how much experience, full or part time, what shift?
  • Is Bell experience required, or preferred?
  • What does a mechanic here do day to day?
  • How many people work at the shop, and what's it like to work there?
  • What's offered — insurance, time off, retirement, training, tool allowance?
  • Should resumes go to a separate email address?
  • Is there an equal opportunity employer statement already in use?

News

  • Is there anything worth posting as a news item? if not, we'll hold the page rather than run it empty
