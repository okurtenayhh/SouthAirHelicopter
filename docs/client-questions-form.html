Website Questions
Please write answers in the spaces

The website is built. Every page exists and works. What's missing is the real information to put in it — right now a lot of the text on the page is a note‑to‑self in a yellow box, waiting on an answer. That's what this is. No deadline, and no need to go in order. Skip anything you don't know or don't want on the site — "I don't know" and "leave that off" are both real answers. A few questions are marked Important — those are the ones where a guess could cause an actual problem, so they're worth a careful answer rather than a quick one.

If nothing else gets answered, these five move the most

  1. Which helicopters is the shop rated on, and what work on each?
  2. What year was the company founded?
  3. Can the NASA work be described in writing?
  4. Can Bell send their seal artwork and written permission?
  5. Can we spend an afternoon at the hangar taking photos?

2Aircraft and what you work on

ImportantWhich helicopter models is the shop rated to work on?
Naming a model the shop isn't rated for is something a customer could act on and make a maintenance decision from. So the page stays empty until this list is confirmed. Please list only what's actually on the certificate.
ImportantFor each model, what work is authorized?
Inspections, airframe, engine, avionics, component work — whatever applies. It can differ by model.
Is the shop Bell‑focused, or does it work across manufacturers?
This changes how the whole page is framed.

3Certifications

ImportantCan Bell provide their seal artwork and written permission to use it?
Bell's mark can't go on the site until Bell sends the artwork and says in writing it's okay. There's an empty space on the page reserved for it. This comes from the Bell account rep rather than the ethics hotline.
What does being a Bell Customer Service Facility actually authorize the shop to do?
Does it cover warranty work? What can this shop do that a shop without the designation can't?
Is "Bell Helicopter Customer Service Facility" still the current term?
That wording came off the business card. Bell dropped "Helicopter" from the name in 2018, so the site may be using a retired term.
How long has the shop held the Bell designation, and what's required to keep it?
Recurring audits, factory training, specific tooling?
ImportantWhat ratings are on FAA Repair Station certificate #XRIR622K, and are there any limitations?
Airframe, powerplant, radio, instrument, accessory — and which classes. This is printed on the certificate itself, so a photo or copy of it would answer this completely.
Does the shop hold any other certifications or authorizations?
Anything else worth listing at the bottom of the site.

4NASA

ImportantWhat exactly does South Air Helicopters do in connection with NASA?
This needs to be specific and factual — for example, "provides helicopter maintenance under contract to [specific program]." What it can't do is suggest NASA endorses or partners with the company, because NASA has firm rules about vendors using their name that way.
ImportantWhich NASA center, facility, or program?
What year did the NASA work start, and how did it come about?
ImportantHow much of this is okay to say publicly?
If any of it is under contract terms that limit what can be advertised, we need to know before the page goes up. Also: does NASA need to review anything that mentions them?
The site currently describes this as a "partnership." Is that accurate, and is it how you want it described?
