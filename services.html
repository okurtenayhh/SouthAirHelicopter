Services

What We Do

Maintenance, repair and inspection on Bell helicopters at Pearland Regional Airport. Every job is quoted on its own, because no two aircraft come in needing the same thing.

Our Services

Helicopter Services We Provide

We're a maintenance shop. Aircraft come to us for scheduled work, repair, inspection and overhaul. We don't fuel, we don't tie down, and we don't fly. Not sure whether we work on your aircraft? Call the office at 281.648.5187 and ask.

Scheduled Maintenance

Routine and scheduled maintenance on Bell helicopters, carried out at our facility on Pearland Regional Airport.

Airframe & Engine Repair

Field maintenance and repair, plus component overhaul on Bell helicopters. Engine overhauls go out to an overhaul facility; we handle removal, installation and return to service.

Avionics & Systems

Avionics work is handled through an avionics shop here on the field, so it can be coordinated alongside the rest of the job.

Pre-Purchase & Annual Inspections

Annual and pre-purchase inspections under FAA Repair Station certificate #XRIR622K.

Bell Warranty Work

As a Bell Customer Service Facility since 1981, we're authorized to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts.

What We Don't Do

No fueling (fuel is on the airport), no tie-downs beyond aircraft in for maintenance, and no flying other than ground runs. Engine overhauls are sent out.

The Authorization

Bell Customer Service Facility

The designation authorizes us to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts. That is the practical difference between us and a shop without it: if your aircraft or a part is still under Bell warranty, the work can happen here rather than somewhere further away.

South Air has held the designation since 1981. Keeping it is not a one-time approval. It takes recurring audits, factory training for our mechanics, and the model-specific tooling Bell requires.

How Pricing Works

Helicopter work doesn't have a menu price. What a job costs depends on the airframe, its hours, what the inspection turns up, and what parts are available, so we quote each one.

What to Send Us

The scope of work you need performed. Tell us what's wrong or what's due, and we'll ask for the aircraft details we need.

What Happens Next

Talk to Us Directly
