The Authorization

Bell Customer Service Facility

The designation authorizes us to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts. That is the practical difference between us and a shop without it: if your aircraft or a part is still under Bell warranty, the work can happen here rather than somewhere further away.

South Air has held the designation since 1981. Keeping it is not a one-time approval — it requires recurring audits, factory training for our mechanics, and the model-specific tooling Bell requires for the airframes we are rated on.
