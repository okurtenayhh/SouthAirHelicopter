Since 1979

Working on Helicopters Since 1979

Robert H. Mitchell founded South Air Helicopters in October 1979. Two years later, in 1981, the shop became a Bell Customer Service Facility. Today it sits on the north side of Pearland Regional Airport, the field once known as Clover Field.

The work has been kept in house since the start, by people who tend to stay. Bell service facilities have come and gone across the region over the years. South Air is still here, still working for customers who keep coming back.

Quality and safety is our priority, and our history reflects it.

Authorizations

A Bell Customer Service Facility

South Air has been a Bell Customer Service Facility since 1981. The designation authorizes us to perform warranty work on new Bell aircraft and parts, and it isn't a one-time approval. It's kept through recurring audits, factory training for our mechanics, and model-specific tooling.

We're also an FAA Certified Repair Station, #XRIR622K.
